🎵 momplaymp3 Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

momplaymp3 ("the app") is a music player for MP3 files that you already have on your device. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

The app does not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, or any other personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, and no tracking.

Your music files and playlists

When you import MP3 files, the app copies them into its own private storage on your device. Your playlists and song list are saved in that same private storage. This data is never uploaded or sent anywhere by the app. Deleting the app removes it.

Access to files

The app only reads files and folders that you choose yourself through the iOS Files picker. If you pick a folder, the app remembers it on your device so you can refresh your songs later. The app cannot see any other files on your device.

Network and third parties

The app works offline and does not connect to the internet. It does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software.

Children's privacy

The app is friendly for children and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
